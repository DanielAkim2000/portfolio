# The project

ft_transcendence is the last project of the 42 common core, built as a team of five. We made a multiplayer web platform around **3D online chess** and the **two physical foosball (babyfoot) tables of 42 Paris**.

On the chess side, you can challenge a friend (from the lobby or straight from the chat) or play an AI with four difficulty levels, on a real-time 3D board with rules fully enforced by the server. On the foosball side, the tables stay physical: the app is the digital layer that organises them, with free or busy status, a queue, 1v1 or 2v2 challenges and tournaments.

---

# Stack and architecture

- **Front**: React 19 and Three.js for the 3D board
- **API**: NestJS 11, PostgreSQL through Prisma
- **Real time**: WebSockets (chat, presence, games)
- **Deployment**: containerised application served over HTTPS by nginx, which generates a self-signed certificate on first start

---

# Features

- **Accounts and security**: email sign-up, 42 intra OAuth 2.0 login, password reset by email, hashed and rotating refresh tokens, TOTP two-factor authentication with recovery codes
- **Social**: profile, friends, online presence synced across tabs, private real-time chat (blocking, unread counts, typing indicators, read receipts)
- **Chess**: complete rules (check, mate, stalemate, repetition, insufficient material, resignation), server-validated moves, game history
- **Ranking**: opponent-validated score, server-computed Elo, single-elimination tournaments with random draw and automatic table assignment
- **Gamification**: experience points, levels and seven unlockable badges
- **Administration**: user management, roles, temporary or permanent bans
- **Operations**: public status page, automated PostgreSQL backups with a restore script, data export and account deletion (GDPR)
- **Interface**: English, French and Spanish, light, dark or system theme, customisable per user
